08 · Focus · press Tab here

Focus ring

2px solid --color-focus, 2px offset, on :focus-visible only. The ring color flips with the surface: teal-800 on light, teal-300 on dark. Both exceed 3:1 against their background.

  • 24px grid, 1.5px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills (status glyphs excepted). Sizes 16 / 20 / 24.
